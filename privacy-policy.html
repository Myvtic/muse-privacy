Privacy Policy

Muse Social Sales Agent · Last updated: 19 September 2026

Muse Social Sales Agent ("the Service") helps businesses respond to comments and messages on their own Instagram and Facebook accounts. This policy explains what data the Service handles, why, and how it is protected.

Who operates the Service

The Service is operated by Mohamad Salem, based in Edmonton, Alberta, Canada. Questions about this policy or about data held by the Service can be sent to truemsalem@gmail.com.

What data is collected

When a business connects its account, the Service processes:

  • Account information — the Instagram or Facebook account ID, username, and the access token granted by that business.
  • Public comments — the text, comment ID, timestamp, and the ID and username of the person who left the comment on the connected business's posts.
  • Direct messages — the content of messages exchanged between the business and a customer through the Service.
  • Post context — captions, media IDs, and permalinks of the connected business's own posts, used to identify which product a comment refers to.
  • Business-supplied content — product names, prices, availability, and links that the business chooses to provide.
  • Operational records — a log of actions taken (which reply was sent, when, and whether it succeeded), kept so that no customer is replied to twice.

The Service does not collect payment card details, government identification numbers, or the contents of any account other than those a business has explicitly connected.

How the data is used

  • To identify what a customer is asking and reply on the business's behalf.
  • To send a single private reply (direct message) in response to a comment, where the platform permits it.
  • To escalate to the business owner anything the Service should not answer automatically.
  • To produce summaries and demand signals for the business owner — for example, how many people asked about a particular product.

Data is not sold, rented, or shared with advertisers. It is not used to train machine learning models. It is not shared with third parties except the service providers listed below, which are used to operate the Service itself.

Service providers

  • Meta Platforms — the source of comment and message data, via the Instagram and Facebook APIs.
  • Supabase — database hosting.
  • Inngest — background job processing.

How long data is kept

Comment and message records are retained while the business's account is connected, and are deleted within 30 days of disconnection or of a deletion request. Access tokens are deleted immediately on disconnection.

Security

Access tokens are encrypted at rest and are never exposed to any third-party agent or client application. All traffic between the Service and Meta's APIs uses HTTPS. Incoming webhook requests are cryptographically verified before being processed.

Your rights

A connected business may request access to, correction of, or deletion of its data at any time by emailing truemsalem@gmail.com. Requests are actioned within 30 days. A business can also revoke the Service's access at any time from its Instagram or Facebook settings, which stops all further processing.

If you commented or messaged a business that uses the Service and want your data removed, email the address above with the account and approximate date, and it will be deleted.

Children

The Service is intended for businesses and is not directed at anyone under 18. It does not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted at this URL with an updated date above. Connected businesses will be notified of material changes by email.
